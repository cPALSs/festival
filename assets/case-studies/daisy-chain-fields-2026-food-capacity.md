# Daisy Chain Fields 2026 — food capacity

**Full case study:** [LNY Research — Daisy Chain Fields 2026 Food Capacity](../../../../Programs/Lunar%20New%20Year/Research/Case%20Study%20-%20Daisy%20Chain%20Fields%202026%20Food%20Capacity.md)

---

## Context

Inaugural **Olivia Rodrigo** one-day festival · **Aug 29, 2026** · Great Park Irvine · **~45K** sold out · **10:30 AM–10 PM** · outside food banned (prepack snacks OK).

---

## What happened

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Food + drink vendors (announced) | **50+** |
| Open food (estimate) | **~35–45** |
| Bars (estimate) | **~10–15** |
| Food wait | **1–2 hr** |
| Bar wait | **none** |
| Field ask | **~4× open food** |

---

## Lesson

**Captive concert demand ≠ family fair demand.** Naive `attendees ÷ vendors` made Daisy look **sparser** than EGLNY 2026 (36 food @ 6K) — yet Daisy **guest experience failed on food** while EGLNY **vendor economics failed on oversupply**.

Open-food planning @ 45K captive day: **~135–150** cook points (**~100–120 more** than fielded), **bars flat**.

---

## EGLNY implication

Keep [Market Modeling](https://github.com/cPALSs/cPALSs/blob/main/Programs/Lunar%20New%20Year/2027/Business%20Development/Market%20Modeling.md) **11 open + 5 drinks @ 9K** for **40% buy** baseline. Use Daisy as **captive-audience stress case** (70%+ buy → ~13–14 open cook @ 9K), not default booth math.
