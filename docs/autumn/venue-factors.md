*Bao Thien Ngo · cPALSs Festivals Chair · August 2026*

Date pick and **site** pick are coupled — inventory differs by facility — but they use different evidence. This page is a **suitability checklist** any Mid-Autumn / lantern host can fork before locking a park or hall.

**Related:** [Weekend booking — decision engine](weekend-booking-decision-engine.html) · [2027 example](weekend-booking-2027-example.html) · [Autumn market research](market-research.html)

---

## Hard gates first

1. **Will the operator permit your product** (evening lantern walk, stage, food) on the weekends you want?  
2. **Local blackouts** — e.g. Cosumnes CSD has made clear that **Elk Grove Regional Park** is not appropriate for Mid-Autumn / GLF-class events in the **~one month before and after Giant Pumpkin Festival** (GPF typically early October on that campus). Do not fight GPF on the same site.  
3. **Booking lead time** — many CSD park weekends open ~**365 days** out.

Everything below is soft suitability research — parking, shade, buildings — not a substitute for a yes/no from parks or City facilities.

## Candidate sites (example set — Greater Sacramento)

| Site | Typical role | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Neighborhood CSD park (e.g. Derr-Okamoto) | Lantern evening / park layout | Candidate — confirm inventory |
| Community hall / Town Hall (e.g. Laguna Town Hall) | Prior Mid-Autumn home for some hosts | Candidate — hall + parking rules differ |
| Elk Grove Regional Park | LNY / GPF campus | **Often no-go** in GPF ± ~1 month |
| Civic campus (e.g. District 56) | Peer Mid-Autumn hosts | Usually a **lane** choice, not automatic default |

Add your own rows; do not invent fees or capacity until the facility confirms.

## Factors to weigh

### Access & parking

- Public parking capacity and overflow  
- Walk from stalls to gates  
- Evening egress (exit crush after dusk close)  
- Drop-off / transit / ADA routes  

### Buildings & infrastructure

- Indoor hall (rain, elders, registration, storage)  
- Restrooms and lighting  
- Power and water for stage + food + LED lanterns  
- Load-in paths for canopy / trucks  

### Family landscape (lantern product)

- Playgrounds (early evening kids draw)  
- Picnic tables / seating  
- **Shade / tree groves** (September heat before dusk)  
- Open lawn or loop for a **dusk procession**  
- Flat stage pad and neighbor noise  

### Ops & policy

- Permit hours / noise curfew vs your public close  
- Alcohol / security rules  
- Fee vs in-kind history  
- Same-weekend peer conflict (another Mid-Autumn in the same city)

## How to use this

1. Shortlist 2–3 sites that clear hard gates.  
2. Field-walk each (afternoon heat + dusk walk).  
3. Pull official facility maps / special-event packets.  
4. Score **TBD → partial → strong** per factor — prefer notes over memory.  
5. Name the site, **then** ask inventory for your moon-relative Saturdays ([booking engine](weekend-booking-decision-engine.html)).
