## Soft-rank snapshot (provisional)

### Community collisions (2026 pattern → 2027)

From the 2026 autumn landscape (moon-relative to Fri Sep 25 / Sat Sep 26):

| Rel | 2026 | What landed |
|----:|------|-------------|
| **−1** | Sep 19 | Densest cluster — CAAPS Sat · CAIMH Sun · campus Mon · parish est. |
| **0** | Sep 26 | Lunar weekend largely open for a park-scale family fair |
| **+1** | Oct 3 | **Giant Pumpkin Festival** (~75K ‡ claim) + early-Oct plaza Mid-Autumn |

Peers may move in 2027 — refresh when flyers post.

### School / holiday

EGUSD traditionally starts mid-August — by early September families are weeks in. **Sat Sep 4, 2027 = Labor Day weekend** (Mon Sep 6) — treat as holiday travel. UC Davis / VSA metro presence before instruction is **unknown** — do not score it as upside.

### Rain vs heat

Sep–early Oct Sac wet-weekend rates ~**0–6%** — rain is a **wash**. Hourly means show peak heat ~**4 PM**; Sep 4 is the heat outlier (~90°F mean at 4 PM). Lantern hours are workable on all five.

### Daylight (Elk Grove area)

Civil dusk ~**7:58 PM** (Sep 4) → **7:13 PM** (Oct 2). Inside a fixed **2:30–9:30** public window, post-dusk lantern time grows from ~1.5 h to ~2.3 h.

## Provisional recommendation

**Prefer Saturday, September 18, 2027** (week 0) — best moon proximity, clearest of the 2026-style −1 cluster and of Giant Pumpkin weekend, no Labor Day, cooler mid-Sep afternoon than early September.
