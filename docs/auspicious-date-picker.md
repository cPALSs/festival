Picking a festival weekend is easy to turn into a date fight. This toolkit piece is a **reusable method**: lock what the **venue will allow**, then rank remaining weekends with evidence — Tết- or moon-relative labels, draw collisions, weather climatology — not vibes.

Autumn hosts also use [Venue factors](autumn/venue-factors.html) before asking the park for inventory.

## Hard rule (both seasons)

**Only score weekends the venue will permit.** Preference and research sit downstream of inventory. If the available set is empty, escalate the venue — do not invent a date the park cannot hold.
