## The choice (2027)

**LNY Day:** Saturday **February 6, 2027**  
**Super Bowl LXI:** Sunday **February 14** (~afternoon PT) — first Super Bowl on **Valentine’s Day**

After Cosumnes CSD confirmed park inventory (August 2026), only **two** Sat–Sun weekends were available:

| | **Option A** | **Option B** |
|---|--------------|--------------|
| **Weekend** | **Feb 13–14** | **Feb 27–28** |
| Tet-relative | week +1 | ~week +3 |
| **Saturday** | Story / youth day (Soul Torrent) | Same |
| **Sunday** | **Tết Tailgate** (Super Bowl + Valentine’s) | Full Sunday — e.g. Relay Cup + Youth Culture Day |
| Rain likelihood (ISO wk) | ~**31%** wet weekend (week 6) | ~**36%** wet weekend (week 8) |

Other weekends in the Jan–early March window were **unavailable** — pavilion **weddings**, not “we preferred not to.”

Park weekends here book about **365 days** out. Lock 2027 first; deliberate 2028 next.

## How A and B differ

Inventory already fixed the set. Soft factors only **rank** the two.

### Closer to LNY Day

- **A:** week +1 — better holiday proximity  
- **B:** ~week +3 — farther from Tết

### Private family gatherings

- **A:** higher near-Tet risk (still untested with guests)  
- **B:** lower near-Tet risk (still untested)

### Family plans by age cohort

**No data yet.** What households with toddlers vs elementary vs teens vs multi-gen actually do on week +1 vs +3 is unknown — sports, travel, parties, other parks, rest. A family-oriented product needs that competitive map to capture the market; it does **not** currently tip A vs B.

### Community collisions (draw intensity)

Do not count events — use estimated draw. Prior season (2026) taught that **later is not quieter**:

| Tet-rel (2026) | Weekend | Σ est. draw | What carried it |
|----------------|---------|------------:|-----------------|
| **−2** | Feb 7–8 | ~**5K** | Creative Space + SCNCCA / Arden cluster |
| **−1** | Feb 14–15 | ~**9K** | Capstone 5–6K (official park estimate) + same-day peers + parish evening |
| **0** | Feb 21–22 | ~**0.4K** | Lull |
| **+1** | Feb 28–Mar 1 | ~**12–16K‡** | City of Trees / Mardi Gras (organizer claim 10–20K‡) |
| **+2** | Mar 7–8 | ~**1K** | Ticketed concert (imputed) |
| **+3** | — | ~**0** | Clear |

‡ = claim / honesty gap — still useful as “this weekend can dwarf leftovers.”

For **2027** (planning estimates on the landscape sheet — not confirmed):

- Warm-up peers mostly land ~**Jan 30** (clear of A and B)
- **Cache Creek** concert estimated **Sat Feb 27** — same Saturday as Option B’s youth day  
- **Mardi Gras** twin season **TBD** (Fat Tuesday Feb 9) — could pressure A or B depending on when they announce

### Faith-based callout

Sacramento Martyrs Hội Chợ pattern stays week **−1 or 0** — neither A nor B is the usual slot. Confirm when 2027 bulletins post. About **77%** of Vietnamese American adults identify with a religion ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/race-and-ethnicity/2024/08/06/vietnamese-americans-a-survey-data-snapshot/)) — faith calendars are mainstream, not niche.

### Super Bowl + Valentine’s (Option A Sunday)

Mainstream interest is already supported (~⅔ U.S. Super Bowl reach; ~53–56% celebrate Valentine’s). Open questions: **Tailgate at the park vs leave-by-~2 for home**, and same-day game-vs-date tradeoffs. Default product on A Sunday = **capture (Tailgate)**, not flee.

### School calendars

- **A:** weekend before **Presidents Day** Mon Feb 15 (family travel risk); no national SAT/ACT that Saturday  
- **B:** **ACT national date Sat Feb 27** — direct clash with Saturday youth programming; college finals are not a February factor for Sac State / UC Davis

### Youth orgs (VSA / TNTT / GDPT)

School calendars do not say what these groups **do**. Fair prior: Tet-geared. Mode unknown (dance vs day-of logistics vs free). Until surveyed with campus partners (e.g. UVSA), both weekends **underweight** youth-org reality.

### Sunday ops readiness

- **A:** Tailgate already designed for Super Bowl Sunday  
- **B:** Full Sunday stack (Relay Cup + Youth Culture Day) still needs build

## Provisional recommendation

**Book Option A — February 13–14, 2027.**

Why, on current evidence:

1. Closer to LNY Day  
2. Sunday product ready (Tailgate) for Super Bowl + Valentine’s  
3. No national ACT on Saturday  
4. Weather roughly tied / slightly favors A  
5. “Prefer not Super Bowl” is organizer preference — mainstream interest shares do not support fleeing without Tailgate-vs-home evidence  

**Caveats (re-score when these land):**

- Family weekend plans by **age cohort** across Tet-season weekends (research TODO — no data yet)  
- Youth-org activity mode (VSA / TNTT / GDPT) still unverified  
- Guest preference: park Tailgate vs home TV  
- Peer 2027 dates (especially Cache Creek, Mardi Gras, District56 / SCNCCA) still estimates  

Steering still locks the date. After lock: confirm with the park, update public copy, and start **2028** deliberation on the 365-day clock.
